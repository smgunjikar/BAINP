# Official challenge requirements -> evidence plan

Challenge: Population Health / Protecting Brain Health Earlier (Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge 2026).

| Organizer outcome | Product module | Demonstration and evidence | Validation remaining |
|---|---|---|---|
| Culturally valid and accessible assessment | NeuroAccess + NeuroCognition | Clear English demo instructions, accommodations specification, non-diagnostic browser task | Localized psychometric validation and real demographic usability cohorts |
| Longitudinal change detection | NeuroTwin + NeuroGuard | Actual persisted sessions and descriptive timelines; synthetic variation cases | Repeat reliability, practice effects, qualified change thresholds, longitudinal cohorts |
| Integrated risk context | NeuroSense, NeuroPulse, NeuroVision, clinical observations | Separate OASIS/PADS/EEG research tracks; synthetic risk-factor context for workflow | Linked authorized modalities, consented clinical and genomic imports |
| Personalized prevention and timely care | NeuroSim + CareLoop | Fictional cases and care statuses; guideline-linked future explanations | Clinical pathway deployment, intervention studies and referral outcomes |
| Safety and human oversight | NeuroGuard, consent and clinician roles | Seven scripted synthetic cases, acute-precedence tests and clear no-diagnosis boundary | Formal clinical rules, regulatory analysis, robust authentication and clinical review |
| Validation/local performance | NeuroSignal ARENA | API tests, synthetic task consistency and planned held-out research analyses | Clinical accuracy, local language/age/education stratification, false referral and time-to-care evaluation |

**No result from synthetic cases can be reported as clinical sensitivity/specificity.**
