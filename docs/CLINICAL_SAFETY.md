# Safety boundary

**No clinical use** for this starter project. The initial UI and API are openly accessible localhost demo components, intentionally without production authentication. Never enter or upload identifiable patient details. Never expose the app to the public internet with real health records.

The `interpret_case` rules apply to seven fictional cases for software testing. They are NOT validated triage rules. In a real clinical workflow, sudden confusion, focal neurological symptoms and other acute warning signs require urgent professional/emergency care, regardless of sensor results. Professional protocols must determine exact escalation policy.

The product cannot diagnose or rule out Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, ALS, MS, vascular disease, depression or other causes from wearable signals or cognitive toy tasks. Structural MRI, EEG and oxidative-stress research measures are not universal diagnostic markers.

Before any real-world study: institutional/ethics review, informed consent, appropriate assessment rights and translations, privacy/security governance, professional oversight, intended-use classification, model risk documentation and bias/fairness testing.
