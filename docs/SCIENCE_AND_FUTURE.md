# Future Research and Translational Product

## Evidence levels

**Measure -> characterize -> validate -> intervene -> demonstrate benefit.** Not all disease mechanisms cause measurable peripheral changes, and oxidative stress is neither a universal root cause nor a proven wearable signal. Work with licensed clinicians and domain researchers before interpreting biomedical data.

## 2026 hackathon

Accessible cognitive task prototype, synthetic quality-aware longitudinal history, separate OASIS and PADS research, limited EEG protocol/replay concept, CareLoop, software benchmark, research methods/limitations and a compelling live demo.

## 2027 feasibility and partnerships

Regional clinical/academic partnerships; validated and culturally adapted tests; per-device reliability; more longitudinal cohorts; ethics-approved usability study; real consent/privacy controls, clinical pathways and cost-of-care research.

## 2027–2029 validation

Clinical prospective cohort, repeatability under real-world conditions, age/education/language fairness, held-out sites, clinical adjudication, care outcome effectiveness, calibration and real-world benefits. Regulators determine claims and product classification.

## 2029 onward

Clinical Brain Health OS, NeuroTwin Research Cloud, federated population-quality analytics, ear-EEG and suitable wearable interfaces, evidence-supported multimodal biomarker models, clinical biomarkers within their validated indications, and selective oxidative stress sensor R&D after analytics/clinical validation.

## Business distinction

Commercial software should rely only on models and data assets with correct commercial rights. OASIS permissions/restrictions must be separately reviewed and cannot be assumed to transfer to commercial products. No sale or monetization of research patients' data.
