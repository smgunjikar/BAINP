# NeuroSignal ARENA

Benchmark A: synthetic safety precedence and abstention checks. Benchmark B: participant-held-out PADS movement classification. Benchmark C: OASIS MRI quality and descriptive longitudinal analysis. Benchmark D: EEG signal quality and held-out research experiments. Benchmark E: clinical workflow and accessibility; real-world clinical sensitivity/specificity remain future research outcomes.

Pre-register target labels and metrics, split at participant and site levels, test for leakage, do not use diagnosis-defining labels as predictors, report calibration and uncertainty. Simulated scenario pass rates are not diagnostic performance.
