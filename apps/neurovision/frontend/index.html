DICOM, larger scans and segmentation inputs

Use the installed local companion for DICOM series, files above 64 MB, masks and segmentation labels. An installed dcm2niix is required for DICOM conversion.

powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\apps\neurovision\Import-NeuroVision.ps1"
