Wearable integration boundary

Device connection is not configured. Commercial watch/ring support requires the exact device model and an authorized SDK, API or export format.

  • Movement: timestamped acceleration and rotation, units, wrist and sample rate.
  • Cardiovascular: heart-rate observations; ECG/PPG only where the device exposes those signals.
  • Sleep: device-estimated duration, timing and algorithm version.
  • Repeated monitoring: participant consent, device provenance, missingness and comparable recording conditions.

PADS supplies movement training data. Heart, sleep, gait and ring models require separate datasets and validation.
